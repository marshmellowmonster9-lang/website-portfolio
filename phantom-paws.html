Phantom Paws

Stylised, Third-Person Puzzle Shooter

Overview

Phantom Paws is a third-person shooter and puzzle game where players battle mischievous cat ghosts and uncover the secrets of a haunted castle. Developed by the University of Gloucestershire’s MSc Game Development team for the Nintendo Switch, the game was showcased at Insomnia #i72 and will be released on Itch.io.

"Clear silhouettes and readable shapes were key to supporting gameplay."

Key Details

  • Role: Contractor Prop Artist
  • Tools: Blender, Substance Painter, Unity
