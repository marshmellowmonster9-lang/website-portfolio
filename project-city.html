Environment Art — Unreal Engine 5

The Office - 1970s Detective Interior

A 1970s detective office environment focused on environmental storytelling, modular construction, realistic props and cinematic lighting.

Unreal Engine 5 Environment Art Cinematic Lighting

Overview

This project explores how environment art, composition and lighting can communicate narrative without relying on explicit dialogue or exposition. Starting from concept art by Alec Chalmers, I developed the environment from blockout through to final presentation in Unreal Engine 5. I created the environment and props in Blender, textured assets in Substance Painter, then assembled, dressed and lit the scene in Unreal Engine 5.

"The project demonstrate my approach to environment art through asset creation, environmental storytelling and real-time presentation."

Key Details

  • Role: Environment Artist
  • Tools: Unreal Engine 5, Blender, Substance Painter
  • Assets: All assets were created by myself
  • Focus: Modular environments, prop creation, trim sheets, vertex painting, set dressing and lighting

Lighting & Atmosphere

Lighting was used as a primary storytelling and composition tool. High-contrast lighting, volumetric effects and post-processing were combined to establish the noir-inspired atmosphere and direct attention towards key areas of the scene. The environment was also built as a first-person walkthrough, with spatial audio used to support the sense of place through ambient city noise, mechanical sounds and subtle environmental effects.

Hero Prop

The tape recorder was developed as a hero prop and focal point within the environment. The asset uses efficiently laid-out UVs and packed texture maps to maximise texture space while maintaining detailed close-up presentation.

Vertex Painting & Trim Sheets

Trim sheets were used across the architectural elements to create reusable materials and maintain consistency across the environment. Vertex painting was used to create exposed brickwork, adding variation and surface detail to the walls without requiring unique textures for every asset. Decals were also used to break up repetition and add additional surface detail, helping reinforce the aged 1970s setting.

Set Dressing & Environmental Storytelling

The environment was designed to imply a narrative through its props and composition. Paperwork, photographs, weapons and general office clutter were deliberately arranged to suggest an active murder investigation and the behaviour of the occupant. The placement of the hero props also creates visual focal points, helping guide the viewer through the scene while reinforcing the narrative.

Modular Asset Showcase

The environment was constructed using modular architectural pieces alongside bespoke props. This allowed assets to be reused across the scene while maintaining visual variation through materials, vertex painting and set dressing.
