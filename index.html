About Me

I'm a 3D Environment Artist passionate about creating immersive, atmospheric environments and engaging experiences. With strong skills in environment art and level design, I focus on crafting visually compelling spaces that enhance gameplay and storytelling.

I currently work as a 3D Environment Artist at Matt Murton Digital Ltd., where I create real-time environments, design interactive AR experiences, develop visual effects (VFX), and optimise assets for mobile augmented reality applications. Alongside my professional work, I continue to develop personal projects in Unreal Engine 5 and Unity, expanding my skills in environment art, material creation, lighting, optimisation, and interactive environment design.

I graduated with a First-Class BSc (Hons) in Computer Games Design from the University of Gloucestershire. During my studies, I contributed to multiple award-winning projects, including Rogue Directive: Genesis, which received the Best Group Project 2026 award. My independent VR project, Please Wave Back, was awarded Best Independent Project 2026, recognising my work in designing an immersive virtual reality experience that explored presence and interaction.

I'm always looking for opportunities to learn, create impactful art, solve creative and technical challenges, and collaborate with talented teams.
