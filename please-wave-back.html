Environment Art — VR Horror

Please Wave Back VR

A VR horror experience focused on atmosphere, environmental storytelling and player interaction, developed for Meta Quest 3.

Unity 6 Environment Art Interactive Design VR

Overview

Please Wave Back is an independently developed VR horror prototype created in Unity 6 for Meta Quest 3 as part of my final-year dissertation, awarded Best Independent Project 2026 at the University of Gloucestershire.
I developed all aspects of the project including environment art, level design, interactive experience design, lighting, VFX, audio and technical implementation. The project demonstrates my approach to building atmospheric, playable environments while working within the technical constraints of standalone VR.

"The project demonstrates how environment art, interaction and atmosphere can work together to create a cohesive player experience."

Key Notes

  • Role: Environment Artist & Solo Developer
  • Platform: Meta Quest 3
  • Tools: Unity 6, Blender, Substance Designer, Substance Painter, Shader Graph, SpeedTree
  • Focus: Environment Art, Level Design, Lighting, VFX, Audio, VR Interaction Design and Optimisation

Technical breakdown

Technical breakdowns covering environment art, vegetation, lighting and interactive experience design.

Vegetation & Environment Art

The forest environment combines custom assets, vegetation, atlas materials and set dressing to create a cohesive and atmospheric setting.
SpeedTree was used to create and optimise the tree assets, with LODs and billboard techniques used to improve performance using player viewing distances. Vegetation placement was also used to control sightlines and frame areas of interest.

Materials & Surface Detail

Custom ground materials were created in Substance Designer to introduce variation and surface detail while maintaining a consistent visual style. Materials were developed for real-time VR, balancing surface quality with texture memory and performance requirements.

Props & Environmental Storytelling

Bespoke props were modelled, textured and dressed throughout the environment to establish the setting and provide visual points of interest. Prop placement and set dressing were used to support environmental storytelling while maintaining a consistent visual language across the scene.

Lighting & Atmosphere

Lighting was designed to establish mood, control visibility and direct player attention throughout the environment. Dynamic lighting, volumetric fog and atmospheric effects were combined to create depth and controlled visibility. Lightning effects were created using Unity VFX Graph and integrated with the wider environmental systems.

Interactive Experience Design

The experience was designed around exploration, observation and player response, using environment layout and interactive systems to control pacing and tension. The Watcher responds directly to player behaviour, creating a clear cause-and-effect relationship between player actions and the environment. Sightlines, encounters and feedback were designed together to create a cohesive player experience.

VFX & Audio

Real-time VFX and spatial audio were used to make the environment feel reactive and reinforce atmosphere.
Bats, flies, cockroaches, fog and lightning were created using Unity VFX Graph and Unity's particle system, adding motion and unpredictability to the environment, trying to increase a players immersion. Spatial audio, ambient forest sounds and thunder reinforced environmental awareness and complemented the visual design.
