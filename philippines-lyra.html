Unreal Engine Level Design Multiplayer

Overview

Philippines Lyra Map is a multiplayer capture-point level developed in Unreal Engine 5 using the Lyra framework. Inspired by Philippine architecture and environments, the map combines a dense town square with a contrasting coastal dockside area. The level was developed through node mapping, whiteboxing, playtesting and refinement, with a focus on player flow, verticality, cover, traversal and environmental readability.

"The level was developed through repeated playtesting, using player feedback to refine flow, balance and the overall experience."

Technical breakdown

Level design, multiplayer layout, environmental composition and playtesting.

Map Design & Player Flow

The map was planned through node mapping and whiteboxing, establishing capture points, routes and key engagement spaces before visual development. Player flow was refined to balance travel distance, engagement frequency and alternative routes between objectives.

Verticality, Cover & Traversal

Layered combat spaces combine ground-level routes, balconies, rooftops and elevated objectives to create varied engagement distances. Cover placement, jump pads and push pads provide alternative approaches while maintaining clear sightlines and readable combat spaces.

Environmental Composition & Storytelling

Philippine-inspired architecture, landmarks, props and vegetation were used to establish distinct areas and strengthen the map's visual identity. Environmental composition also supports gameplay through recognisable landmarks, visual callouts and readable routes.

Playtesting & Iteration

The map went through three rounds of playtesting, with feedback used to identify issues with spawn camping, traversal, objective positioning and map scale. Subsequent iterations redesigned spawn areas, repositioned capture points and introduced additional routes and verticality to improve multiplayer flow.
