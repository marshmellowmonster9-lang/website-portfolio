Reveal: The Past

Interactive AR experiences that bring heritage places to life through characters, reconstructions, guided trails and playful discovery.

Overview

Reveal: The Past turns heritage interpretation into location-based AR, letting visitors explore stories through their own device in the places where those stories belong. My contribution as the sole 3D artist focused on creating the real-time environments, assets and visual effects that support these interactive experiences.

"Clear silhouettes and readable shapes were key to supporting gameplay."

Key Details

  • Role: Environment Artist
  • Focus: Real-time 3D, AR environments, visual effects
