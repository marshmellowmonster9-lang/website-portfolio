Post-Apocalyptic Rome

This level was part of a paired action-adventure level design project set in post-apocalyptic Rome. Players explore vertical city streets, crumbling buildings, and hidden paths while uncovering the story of a father searching for his son. Using interactive elements, collectibles, and visual cues, the environment conveys narrative and worldbuilding without relying on cutscenes. Principles such as the golden ratio were applied to ensure the level is visually clear, engaging, and immersive.

Overview

Level 1 was developed as part of a paired project for an action-adventure level design module. I created Level 1, focused on environmental storytelling and stealth gameplay, while my partner created Level 2, which emphasized combat. ​ The level is set in post-apocalyptic Rome, blending classical architecture with ruined, overgrown cityscapes. Inspiration was drawn from the Roman Forum, iconic classical structures, and games like The Last of Us, using debris, foliage, and decay to communicate a society in collapse. The goal was to immerse players in a world that tells its story through exploration, environmental cues, and interaction rather than relying solely on dialogue or cutscenes.

“The most important thing was making each location feel like it belonged to a living society, even when the player only sees it for a moment.”

Key Notes

  • Role: Level Designer & Narrative Designer
  • Focus: Narrative flow, atmosphere, POIs, Block-Out
